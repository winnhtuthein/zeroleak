ZeroLeak · Logo System v1

The idea

A page, redacted

The mark is a sheet of paper (the output page in your app) carrying a Z built from three document lines: two blacked-out, one highlighted.

Z, zero, leak: all in one

The gaps between the bars are deliberate. Nothing touches, nothing bleeds. The highlighted "o" in the wordmark is the Zero in ZeroLeak.

Ownable, not a lock

No padlock, shield or keyhole. Every security tool uses those. This is built from your product's own visual language.

Usage

Clear space

Keep free space equal to the height of the yellow "o" chip on every side of the lockup.

Minimum size

Lockup: 120 px wide. Mark: 16 px, switching to the favicon version below 24 px.

Never

Recolour the diagonal, close the gaps between the bars, rotate the mark, or add a padlock.
